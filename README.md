📄 Legal Document Summarization using NLP

🚀 Project Overview
Legal documents such as contracts, agreements, NDAs, and policy documents are often lengthy and time-consuming to review. This project leverages Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques to automatically generate concise summaries while preserving key legal information.

The solution helps legal professionals, compliance teams, and businesses quickly understand important clauses and critical information from large legal documents.



🎯 Objectives
* Automate legal document summarization
* Reduce document review time
* Extract key information from lengthy legal texts
* Improve efficiency in legal analysis workflows
* Demonstrate practical NLP implementation for real-world use cases
